Stage A, the neighbour census. For every analysed token of {{fact:x.viewer.texts}} texts, under each memory and each way of accounting for which text a token comes from: are its 64 nearest tokens in other texts, far away in its own text, or nearby? On a fixed sample, do neighbours share the model's next-token predictions more than matched controls? And which memory construction, if any, should Stage B's through-depth modes use?

Each entry asks one question and answers it in two or three sentences. Every number is read from the stored outputs when the site is built, and every token behind them can be clicked in the Viewer. An independent audit recomputed a sample of them from the raw activations: see How to check. Stage B (through-depth modes, entries E4 and E5) needs a separate approval and is not built yet.
