Depth embedding of tokens

Tested: {{fact:x.depth.model.pythia}} and {{fact:x.depth.model.olmo}}, all {{fact:x.depth.layers}} layers of each (layer 0 is the input; layer l is the output of block l of {{fact:x.depth.blocks}}, one processing step of the model). The texts are the {{fact:x.depth.texts}} Pile documents of the Viewer (the Pile is a public collection of text), named c00 to c31. {{fact:x.d1-depth.sets}} construction sets, each a fixed sample of {{fact:x.depth.set.d32.n}} tokens ({{fact:x.depth.set.doc00.n}} for a single text), built once per model: doc00, doc01, doc02 and doc04 (all of one document each), pairA ({{fact:x.d1-depth.set.paira.ctx}}), pairB ({{fact:x.d1-depth.set.pairb.ctx}}), d04 ({{fact:x.d1-depth.set.d04.ctx}}), d08 ({{fact:x.d1-depth.set.d08.ctx}}), d16 ({{fact:x.d1-depth.set.d16.ctx}}), d32 ({{fact:x.d1-depth.set.d32.ctx}}) and rand ({{fact:x.d1-depth.set.rand.ctx}}: sequences of random tokens whose word pieces were drawn uniformly, with replacement, from those that occur in the {{fact:x.depth.texts}} texts; {{fact:x.depth.unseen_random}} is a further such sequence that no set uses). Three panels, fixed lists of token positions, are shown in every embedding: natural text ({{fact:x.depth.panel.natural}} positions), random tokens ({{fact:x.depth.panel.random}}) and shuffled texts ({{fact:x.depth.panel.shuffled}}; documents {{fact:x.depth.shuffled_sources}} with their tokens put in random order, {{fact:x.depth.first_shuffled}} to {{fact:x.depth.last_shuffled}}). Main numbers: the first {{fact:x.d1-depth.k}} modes, coupling strength t*.

What an embedding is here. A token is one word or piece of a word at one place in one text; the same word piece (for example " the") occurs as many tokens. At every layer the model computes a vector for each token (its activation). Two tokens of a set are linked at a layer if either one is among the other's {{fact:x.depth.knn}} most similar tokens by angle, counting every token tied at the {{fact:x.depth.knn}}th place, so a frequent word piece can have hundreds of links at the input layer; all other pairs keep a tiny background weight. The links are then balanced so that every token's links add up to the same total, and each token is tied to itself at the next layer. A mode is one number for every token at every layer that averages zero over the tokens at each layer and changes as little as possible along the links and from layer to layer. Mode 1 is the smoothest such pattern, mode 2 the next, and so on (technically: eigenvectors of one Laplacian operator over all layers). The first few modes give every token a few coordinates at every layer, in one space shared by all layers. The coupling strength sets how tightly neighbouring layers are tied. Its main value, t*, is the strength at which the smoothest pattern that varies only with depth and the smoothest one that varies across tokens are equally smooth: a crossing point, not a best value. Nothing is trained. A token outside the set can be placed: at each layer it is linked to the set's tokens by the same rule, and its coordinates are solved over all layers together with the embedding held fixed, so its position at one layer also depends on its vectors at the other layers. Panel positions that are already in a set keep their own coordinates. The Depth embedding tab draws these spaces token by token.

How two spaces are compared. Most numbers below are an overlap between two embeddings on the same token positions: after subtracting each layer's average, do the first {{fact:x.d1-depth.k}} coordinates in one embedding vary along the same directions, across those tokens, as the first {{fact:x.d1-depth.k}} in the other? The overlap is 1 when they span the same space, however the axes are rotated, mirrored or scaled inside it, and 0 when they share no direction. Mode 3 of one embedding is never matched with mode 3 of another, and a high overlap does not mean equal distances or the same nearest neighbours. "All layers together" means the layers' rows are stacked into one comparison, not that {{fact:x.depth.layers}} per-layer scores are averaged. These results describe these fixed samples; how much fresh samples would vary was not measured, and no number here is a test of a hypothesis.

1 · What do the leading modes look like?

A mode changes little from one layer to the next, yet in most sets its first and last layers show no consistent alignment; and each mode rests on a minority of the tokens. For the median mode, the average cosine between its values at neighbouring layers is {{fact:x.d1-depth.q1.adj.min}} to {{fact:x.d1-depth.q1.adj.max}} (depending on set and model). The median signed cosine between its values at layer 0 and at layer {{fact:x.depth.last_layer}} lies between {{fact:x.d1-depth.q1.end.min}} and {{fact:x.d1-depth.q1.end.max}} in every embedding except {{fact:x.d1-depth.q1.end.out_who}} ({{fact:x.d1-depth.q1.end.out}}). Signs differ from mode to mode: ignoring the sign, the median size of that cosine is {{fact:x.d1-depth.q1.absend.min}} to {{fact:x.d1-depth.q1.absend.max}}, so single modes can still line up or reverse. The median mode's token support, an effective share (10% means as concentrated as equal weight on 10% of the tokens), is {{fact:x.d1-depth.q1.support.min}} to {{fact:x.d1-depth.q1.support.max}}. In sets that mix texts, the median mode's document share is about {{fact:x.d1-depth.q1.doc.pairs}} or less in the pairs, against {{fact:x.d1-depth.q1.doc.d32.pythia}} ({{fact:x.depth.model.pythia}}) and {{fact:x.d1-depth.q1.doc.d32.olmo}} ({{fact:x.depth.model.olmo}}) in d32, and {{fact:x.d1-depth.q1.doc.rand.min}} to {{fact:x.d1-depth.q1.doc.rand.max}} between rand's sequences.

Read it as: neighbouring layers carry similar patterns; by the last layer most sets show no consistent alignment with the first; and in d32 far more of each mode separates texts than in the pairs.

2 · Do the same tokens get the same space in a different set?

Only partly: neighbouring sets give the same tokens noticeably different spaces. The same {{fact:x.d1-depth.ladder_tokens}} tokens (the natural panel without its c00 positions, so never part of any of these sets) are placed into each set of the ladder doc00 → pairA → d04 → d08 → d16 → d32, and into rand. In {{fact:x.depth.model.pythia}} the overlap from one set to the next is {{fact:x.d1-depth.q2.pythia.doc00_paira}}, {{fact:x.d1-depth.q2.pythia.paira_d04}}, {{fact:x.d1-depth.q2.pythia.d04_d08}}, {{fact:x.d1-depth.q2.pythia.d08_d16}} and {{fact:x.d1-depth.q2.pythia.d16_d32}} along the ladder, and {{fact:x.d1-depth.q2.pythia.d32_rand}} from d32 to rand; in {{fact:x.depth.model.olmo}} the steps along the ladder range from {{fact:x.d1-depth.q2.olmo.min}} to {{fact:x.d1-depth.q2.olmo.max}}, and d32 to rand is {{fact:x.d1-depth.q2.olmo.d32_rand}}. Their size changes as well: the tokens' root-mean-square radius, relative to each set's own tokens (first {{fact:x.d1-depth.kp}} modes), runs from {{fact:x.d1-depth.q2.rr.pythia.min}} to {{fact:x.d1-depth.q2.rr.pythia.max}} along the ladder in {{fact:x.depth.model.pythia}} and from {{fact:x.d1-depth.q2.rr.olmo.min}} to {{fact:x.d1-depth.q2.rr.olmo.max}} in {{fact:x.depth.model.olmo}}, and is about the same in d32 and rand ({{fact:x.d1-depth.q2.rr.pythia.d32}} and {{fact:x.d1-depth.q2.rr.pythia.rand}}; {{fact:x.d1-depth.q2.rr.olmo.d32}} and {{fact:x.d1-depth.q2.rr.olmo.rand}}) although those two spaces overlap little. (Flagged radius: {{fact:x.d1-depth.q2.rr.cut_list}}.) The ladder describes these particular samples, not a clean effect of the number of texts: the sets differ in which texts they contain and in how many tokens each text gives ({{fact:x.d1-depth.set.doc00.each}} from the one text of doc00, {{fact:x.d1-depth.set.paira.each}} each in pairA, down to {{fact:x.d1-depth.set.d32.each}} each in d32).

Read it as: no step keeps the same space; the move from documents to random tokens is among the two largest changes in both models.

3 · Do the two models agree on the same tokens?

Yes, to a fair degree, and much of the overlap survives when the occurrences of each word piece are scrambled. On each set's own tokens, the two models' first {{fact:x.d1-depth.k}} modes overlap {{fact:x.d1-depth.q3.own.min}} to {{fact:x.d1-depth.q3.own.max}} ({{fact:x.d1-depth.q3.stable}} sets; left out: {{fact:x.d1-depth.q3.cut_list}}, whose cutoff at {{fact:x.d1-depth.k}} modes is flagged in one model). Now, in one model, randomly reorder the tokens of each word piece among themselves, so that each token is matched with a random occurrence of the same word piece. A word piece that occurs once cannot move, and a reordering leaves some tokens in place by chance: on average {{fact:x.d1-depth.q3.fixed.min}} to {{fact:x.d1-depth.q3.fixed.max}} of the tokens in the document sets stay matched to their own occurrence. The overlap is then {{fact:x.d1-depth.q3.sameid.min}} to {{fact:x.d1-depth.q3.sameid.max}} in the document sets, {{fact:x.d1-depth.q3.gap.min}} to {{fact:x.d1-depth.q3.gap.max}} lower than with the true matching. Reordering all tokens freely leaves at most {{fact:x.d1-depth.q3.free.max}} (mean of {{fact:x.d1-depth.draws}} reorderings). In rand the word-piece reordering leaves {{fact:x.d1-depth.q3.rand.sameid}} against {{fact:x.d1-depth.q3.rand.own}}, but there {{fact:x.d1-depth.q3.fixed.rand}} of the tokens stay matched on average, so this reference says little.

Read it as: the models' spaces line up well on the same tokens; scrambling occurrences within each word piece lowers the overlap only partly, while leaving some tokens matched; scrambling everything removes it.

4 · Where do tokens of another kind land?

Tokens of a different kind sit at a smaller radius or with a shifted mean, compared with unseen tokens of the set's own kind, and the two models agree less about them. Placed into d32, random tokens have a root-mean-square radius of {{fact:x.d1-depth.q4.pythia.d32.random.unseen_sequence.rr}} ({{fact:x.depth.model.pythia}}) and {{fact:x.d1-depth.q4.olmo.d32.random.unseen_sequence.rr}} ({{fact:x.depth.model.olmo}}) of the set's own, and their mean is shifted from the set's centre by {{fact:x.d1-depth.q4.pythia.d32.random.unseen_sequence.co}} and {{fact:x.d1-depth.q4.olmo.d32.random.unseen_sequence.co}} of the set's radius. For new positions of d32's own texts, the reference, the radius is {{fact:x.d1-depth.q4.pythia.d32.natural.seen_sequence_new_position.rr}} and {{fact:x.d1-depth.q4.olmo.d32.natural.seen_sequence_new_position.rr}} and the shift only {{fact:x.d1-depth.q4.pythia.d32.natural.seen_sequence_new_position.co}} and {{fact:x.d1-depth.q4.olmo.d32.natural.seen_sequence_new_position.co}}. Shuffled texts also have a smaller radius ({{fact:x.d1-depth.q4.pythia.d32.shuffled.unseen_sequence.rr}} and {{fact:x.d1-depth.q4.olmo.d32.shuffled.unseen_sequence.rr}}). Placed into rand, document tokens keep about the usual radius ({{fact:x.d1-depth.q4.pythia.rand.natural.unseen_sequence.rr}} and {{fact:x.d1-depth.q4.olmo.rand.natural.unseen_sequence.rr}}) but their mean is shifted by {{fact:x.d1-depth.q4.pythia.rand.natural.unseen_sequence.co}} and {{fact:x.d1-depth.q4.olmo.rand.natural.unseen_sequence.co}}, against {{fact:x.d1-depth.q4.pythia.rand.random.seen_sequence_new_position.co}} and {{fact:x.d1-depth.q4.olmo.rand.random.seen_sequence_new_position.co}} for new positions of rand's own sequences. A usual radius does not mean a close match: at layer {{fact:x.d1-depth.q4.mid_layer}}, the median cosine between a placed document token and its most similar token of rand is {{fact:x.d1-depth.q4.near.pythia.rand.natural.unseen_sequence}} and {{fact:x.d1-depth.q4.near.olmo.rand.natural.unseen_sequence}}, against {{fact:x.d1-depth.q4.near.pythia.rand.random.seen_sequence_new_position}} and {{fact:x.d1-depth.q4.near.olmo.rand.random.seen_sequence_new_position}} for new positions of rand's sequences (each model's cosines are on its own scale).

Read it as: tokens of the set's own kind sit like the set; random tokens sit closer to the centre and with a shifted mean in a document embedding; document tokens keep a usual radius but a shifted mean in a random-token embedding.

The two models also agree less on placed tokens of another kind. In d32 their overlap is {{fact:x.d1-depth.q4.agree.d32.construction.in_set.v}} on the set's own tokens and {{fact:x.d1-depth.q4.agree.d32.natural.seen_sequence_new_position.v}} on new positions of its texts, but {{fact:x.d1-depth.q4.agree.d32.shuffled.unseen_sequence.v}} on shuffled texts and {{fact:x.d1-depth.q4.agree.d32.random.unseen_sequence.v}} on random tokens (whose word-piece reference, {{fact:x.d1-depth.q4.agree.d32.random.unseen_sequence.ref}}, says little: only {{fact:x.d1-depth.q4.agree.d32.random.unseen_sequence.mov}} of them can be reordered). The same {{fact:x.d1-depth.q4.nat.rand.n}} natural-panel tokens (outside c00) agree less across the models inside rand ({{fact:x.d1-depth.q4.nat.rand.v}}) than inside d32 ({{fact:x.d1-depth.q4.nat.d32.v}}), and inside rand barely more than their word-piece reference ({{fact:x.d1-depth.q4.nat.rand.ref}}).

A check on placement

Placing a token does not rebuild the embedding. To see how much that matters, the natural-panel tokens of d08's own texts were added to d08 and the embedding was solved again at its own t*. This check uses a different score from the overlap above: after one rotation or reflection fitted over all layers on the {{fact:x.depth.set.d08.n}} tokens both solutions contain, it is one minus the squared difference between the two sets of coordinates divided by the refit coordinates' total square (1 = identical; sizes count; it can be negative). For the first {{fact:x.d1-depth.k}} modes the placed tokens score {{fact:x.d1-depth.refit.pythia.16.placed}} ({{fact:x.depth.model.pythia}}) and {{fact:x.d1-depth.refit.olmo.16.placed}} ({{fact:x.depth.model.olmo}}), about as well as the tokens both contain ({{fact:x.d1-depth.refit.pythia.16.shared}} and {{fact:x.d1-depth.refit.olmo.16.shared}}). The refit also moved the links, the balancing and t*, so this is agreement with a second solution, not an exact known answer. (At {{fact:x.d1-depth.k8}} and {{fact:x.d1-depth.k64}} modes the placed scores are {{fact:x.d1-depth.refit.pythia.8.placed}} and {{fact:x.d1-depth.refit.pythia.64.placed}} in {{fact:x.depth.model.pythia}}, {{fact:x.d1-depth.refit.olmo.8.placed}} and {{fact:x.d1-depth.refit.olmo.64.placed}} in {{fact:x.depth.model.olmo}}; {{fact:x.d1-depth.refit.other_cut}} of these {{fact:x.d1-depth.refit.other_n}} cutoffs are flagged: {{fact:x.d1-depth.refit.cut_list}}.)

Caveats

  • Near-tied modes. A cutoff at k modes is flagged when mode k and the next have smoothness scores (eigenvalues) within {{fact:x.depth.tie_pct}} of each other: such modes can mix under small changes in the data, so a cut between them may be arbitrary. The {{fact:x.depth.tie_pct}} line is the spec's display rule, not a proof. Of the {{fact:x.d1-depth.bands.k16.n}} comparison records in the release (both strengths, all groups of tokens), {{fact:x.d1-depth.bands.k16.cut}} are flagged at {{fact:x.d1-depth.k}} modes and {{fact:x.d1-depth.bands.k64.cut}} at {{fact:x.d1-depth.k64}}. Flagged values are drawn open and left out of the ranges quoted for questions 2 and 3. The flags apply to the embeddings built over all layers; question 1's summaries include modes inside near-tied groups, and the layer-by-layer curves are not flagged.
  • Some per-layer overlaps are not defined. At t*, {{fact:x.d1-depth.undef.tstar.count}} of {{fact:x.d1-depth.undef.tstar.total}} per-layer values of the between-model overlap (in {{fact:x.d1-depth.undef.tstar.sets}}) are not reported, because at that layer, on those tokens, one model's first {{fact:x.d1-depth.k}} coordinates do not give {{fact:x.d1-depth.k}} independent directions (one is tiny next to the largest). At t*/16 it is {{fact:x.d1-depth.undef.tstar_div16.count}} of {{fact:x.d1-depth.undef.tstar_div16.total}}, in all {{fact:x.d1-depth.undef.tstar_div16.nsets}} sets; with the looser tie, modes tend to concentrate in fewer layers.
  • t* differs a lot between sets. It is computed for each set and model: from {{fact:x.d1-depth.tstar.pythia.min}} to {{fact:x.d1-depth.tstar.pythia.max}} in {{fact:x.depth.model.pythia}} and from {{fact:x.d1-depth.tstar.olmo.min}} to {{fact:x.d1-depth.tstar.olmo.max}} in {{fact:x.depth.model.olmo}}. The two lowest in each model are doc02 and pairB, the same two sets whose per-layer overlaps are undefined at t*. Sets are therefore compared at different absolute strengths.
  • The reordering references are not chance levels. A word piece that occurs once cannot move, and a random reordering leaves some tokens in place, so the word-piece reference mixes matched and reordered tokens; it is not an estimate of how much agreement the word piece causes. Where under {{fact:x.d1-depth.weak_pct}} of a group can move, it says little (an open diamond in the figures).
  • One sample of each set, two models, {{fact:x.depth.texts}} texts. There are no error bars across samples: lines in the figures span modes or reorderings. The texts are the beginnings of long Pile documents, as in Stage A.
  • Not done. No memory kernel: tokens are compared by their activations at each layer, with no extra comparison of their pasts as in Stage A (the activations themselves reflect earlier text). The site data and the Depth embedding tab carry the first {{fact:x.depth.modes_shown}} of the {{fact:x.depth.modes_kept}} modes kept. The looser strength t*/16 is summarised only in question 1 and the caveats; the Depth embedding tab shows both.
