Caveats

  • Controls are matched on text, current token and distance band only. Anything else a neighbour shares with its query (its own past, say) is part of the advantage by design.
  • Coverage limits the claim. Frequent tokens find controls easily; rare ones often do not, so strata differ in how much they say. Uncovered strata are left out of the score, not filled in.
  • Only the top 20 predictions are compared, so overlap below the 20th token is invisible; the retained mass is stored per stratum.
  • The score is descriptive. It never chooses Stage B's construction (E3) and is not a validation of it; it can add at most one extra example.
