Are neighbours prediction-concordant beyond matched controls?

Tested: a fixed sample of query tokens (per text, 64 in its last 512 positions and 32 earlier), each with its 64 nearest neighbours, against controls matched on the neighbour's text, current token and distance band; the model's own top-20 next-token predictions; layers 4, 8, 12 and 16, both models, both readings. The lead figure is layer 8, cosine reading; the answer's numbers are means over the four layers, corpus mean, no mask.

With no memory, a token's neighbours share the model's most likely next token {{fact:x.e2-concordance.m1.mean.mem_none.ratio}} times as often as controls matched on text and current token ({{fact:x.e2-concordance.m1.mean.mem_none.top1}} against {{fact:x.e2-concordance.m1.mean.mem_none.top1_control}} in {{fact:x.e2-concordance.m1.name}}, {{fact:x.e2-concordance.m2.mean.mem_none.ratio}} times in {{fact:x.e2-concordance.m2.name}}), and the advantage shrinks steadily with memory in both models, to about nothing at the whole prefix (concordance score {{fact:x.e2-concordance.m1.mean.mem_none.score}} with no memory, {{fact:x.e2-concordance.m1.mean.mem_64.score}} at memory 64, {{fact:x.e2-concordance.m1.mean.mem_whole.score}} at the whole prefix). Each control shares its neighbour's text, current token and, within a text, its distance band; nothing else is held fixed and nothing causal is claimed. Neighbours within 64 positions never found enough controls ({{fact:x.e2-concordance.m1.near_covered}} of {{fact:x.e2-concordance.m1.near_strata}} such strata covered), so the score leaves them out ({{fact:x.e2-concordance.m1.mean.mem_64.near_left_out}} of all neighbour pairs at memory 64, {{fact:x.e2-concordance.m1.mean.mem_whole.near_left_out}} at the whole prefix), and that is why most settings are ineligible for ranking ({{fact:x.e2-concordance.m1.all_eligible}} of {{fact:x.e2-concordance.m1.all_settings}} are eligible in {{fact:x.e2-concordance.m1.name}}).

Caveats

  • auditThe first run of this readout failed the independent audit. An int32 overflow in exp1/readout.py (the lookup key of the prediction overlap) recorded zero overlap for every neighbour or control in texts 24 to 31 and the end of text 23. It was fixed with 64-bit keys and two regression tests, the readout and selection were re-run, and the audit then passed. The scores rose; top-1 rates, coverage and the choice of Stage B's construction did not change. See How to check.
  • Controls are matched on text, current token and distance band only. Anything else a neighbour shares with its query (its own past, say) is part of the advantage by design.
  • Coverage limits the claim. Frequent tokens find controls easily; rare ones often do not, so strata differ in how much they say. Uncovered strata are left out of the score, not filled in.
  • Only the top 20 predictions are compared, so overlap below the 20th token is invisible; the retained mass is stored per stratum.
  • The score is descriptive. It never chooses Stage B's construction (E3) and is not a validation of it; it can add at most one extra example.
