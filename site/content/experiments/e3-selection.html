What does the screen select for Stage B, and why?

Tested: {{fact:x.e3-selection.candidates}} candidate memory constructions (memory 2, 8, 64 or whole × cosine or dot reading × corpus or own-text mean × no mask or the window), both models, all {{fact:x.e3-selection.sites}} measured layers, on the rows Stage B would solve: the sampled suffix queries against every token in the last 512 positions of every text. The rules were fixed before any number existed.

The fixed rule picked {{fact:x.e3-selection.m}} as Stage B's memory construction, with {{fact:x.e3-selection.l}} as its local counterpart; {{fact:x.e3-selection.passing}} of the {{fact:x.e3-selection.candidates}} candidates passed in both models. The rule looks first at candidates that subtract the corpus mean and forbid nothing; no cosine candidate passes there (memory 2 stays too close to no memory, memory 8 meets all four conditions together at only {{fact:x.e3-selection.cand.cosine_c_8_none.m1.passing}} of {{fact:x.e3-selection.m1.name}}'s {{fact:x.e3-selection.sites}} layers where {{fact:x.e3-selection.required}} are needed, and at the longer memories more than a quarter of the walk weight sits within 64 positions at most of those layers), and of the two dot candidates that do, memory 64 has the smaller overlap with the text-only inputs ({{fact:x.e3-selection.cand.dot_c_64_none.worst}} against {{fact:x.e3-selection.cand.dot_c_8_none.worst}} at worst). The screen weighs each row by walk weight over the last 512 tokens of every text, which spreads well beyond the 64 nearest: for the chosen construction {{fact:x.e3-selection.m.m1.screen_mass_other}} of that weight lies in other texts in {{fact:x.e3-selection.m1.name}} ({{fact:x.e3-selection.m.m2.screen_mass_other}} in {{fact:x.e3-selection.m2.name}}), against {{fact:x.e3-selection.m.m1.screen_top_other}} of the 64 nearest on the same rows and {{fact:x.e3-selection.m.m1.census_other}} in the full census.

Caveats

  • The screen is geometric. Passing says the neighbourhood is not mostly nearby, not memoryless and not text alone; it says nothing yet about through-depth modes.
  • Condition 1 hardly selects. It held at every layer in both models for {{fact:x.e3-selection.cond1.cosine_c_none_full}} of the {{fact:x.e3-selection.cond1.cosine_c_none}} cosine corpus-mean candidates without a mask, because the walk weight is very flat: at the whole prefix {{fact:x.e3-selection.flat.m1.walk_other}} of it lies in other texts in {{fact:x.e3-selection.m1.name}} while {{fact:x.e3-selection.flat.m1.top_other}} of the 64 nearest on the same rows do.
  • What the pick does not show: its neighbours share the model's predictions with matched controls less than its no-memory counterpart's do (concordance score {{fact:x.e3-selection.m.m1.score}} against {{fact:x.e3-selection.l.m1.score}} in {{fact:x.e3-selection.m1.name}}, {{fact:x.e3-selection.m.m2.score}} against {{fact:x.e3-selection.l.m2.score}} in {{fact:x.e3-selection.m2.name}}, means over the readout layers; E2). The screen is geometric and does not use this.
  • Thresholds were fixed in advance (0.25 and 0.5); a candidate close to a threshold is not singled out.
  • Groups dropped by the spending cap ({{fact:x.e3-selection.dropped}} here) would be greyed in the table and could not pass.
