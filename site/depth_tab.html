Tokens moving through the layers, in one shared space

Each dot is one token of one text. Its position is two of its coordinates in an embedding built from all {{fact:x.depth.layers}} layers of a model at once, so every layer is drawn in the same space and the same token is the same dot at every layer. Depth plays the role of time: move the layer slider, or press Play, and watch the tokens move from the input (layer 0) to the last block (layer {{fact:x.depth.last_layer}}).

A dot is a token

One dot is one token: one position in one text (a word or a piece of a word, as the model splits text). Hover over a dot (tap on a phone) to read the token with the {{fact:x.depth.context}} tokens before and after it and the text it comes from. A token keeps its dot at every layer, so the slider moves dots, it never swaps them.

Layer: the slider and Play

Each model reads a token through {{fact:x.depth.layers}} layers, numbered 0 to {{fact:x.depth.last_layer}}: layer 0 is the token's input vector and layer l the output of block l of {{fact:x.depth.blocks}}. The slider picks the layer; ← and → step one layer; Play runs from 0 to {{fact:x.depth.last_layer}}. Between two layers dots are drawn part of the way along a straight line, for the eye only: nothing was computed in between. The bars above the slider show how much of the two plotted modes sits at each layer.

The two axes are two modes

The embedding gives every token {{fact:x.depth.modes_kept}} coordinates at every layer, called modes and numbered k = 1, 2, …; this page carries the first {{fact:x.depth.modes_shown}}. A mode is one pattern over all (token, layer) pairs at once, computed from which tokens lie close to which: at each layer every token is linked to its {{fact:x.depth.knn}} nearest by the angle between their activation vectors, and each layer is tied to the next. Mode 1 is the smoothest pattern (it changes least between linked tokens and between neighbouring layers), and each later mode is a little less smooth. Because a mode spans every layer, its value at layer 5 and at layer 6 are the same coordinate, which is what lets a dot move continuously. Choose the two plotted modes under Axes. The sign of a mode is arbitrary (flipping it mirrors the plot). Each axis is scaled to its own mode and kept the same at every layer; a few far-out tokens are drawn on the frame's edge and counted (see Axis limits).

Construction set

The tokens an embedding is built from: a fixed sample of positions from one or more texts. The dots are this set's tokens. Each set is built separately for each model, so changing the set changes the space. The texts are the {{fact:x.depth.texts}} Pile documents of the Viewer, numbered c00 to c31, and {{fact:x.depth.random_sequences}} sequences of random tokens, {{fact:x.depth.first_random}} to {{fact:x.depth.last_random}}. Placed tokens: panels and paths

A token outside the set can still be given coordinates: it is compared with the set's tokens at every layer and placed where its links put it, without changing the embedding. Because the layers are tied, its position at one layer also depends on its other layers. A panel is a fixed sample placed into every set: natural ({{fact:x.depth.panel.natural_per_text}} tokens from each of the {{fact:x.depth.texts}} documents, {{fact:x.depth.panel.natural}} in all), random ({{fact:x.depth.panel.random}} from the random sequences, including {{fact:x.depth.unseen_random}}, which no set uses) and shuffled ({{fact:x.depth.panel.shuffled}} from documents {{fact:x.depth.shuffled_sources}} with their tokens put in random order, {{fact:x.depth.first_shuffled}} to {{fact:x.depth.last_shuffled}}). Each panel token is marked as in the set (its coordinates are the set's own), a new position of a text in the set, or a text the set never saw. A path is every token of one text joined in reading order ({{fact:x.depth.paths}}; for d32 and rand only).

Coupling strength

How strongly each layer is tied to the next. t* is the strength at which the first pattern that varies only along depth (the same for every token) and the first pattern that varies across tokens get the same smoothness score: a crossing point, not a best value. t*/16 is a sixteenth of it, a much looser tie under which a mode can change more from layer to layer. t* is found separately for each model and set; its value is in each plot's caption.

Colour

Colour is for reading only; nothing is built from it. Text: the document or random sequence a token comes from (up to {{fact:x.depth.text_colours}} at a time; with more, click names in the key to choose which). Position: where in its text the token sits, from position 1 (pale) to {{fact:x.depth.text_tokens}} (deep). Frequency: how many times the token's id (the same word piece) occurs in the construction set, on a log scale. Panel group: in the set, new position, or unseen text (with a panel shown).

Flags on a mode

Near-tied: neighbouring modes whose eigenvalues (their smoothness scores) differ by under {{fact:x.depth.tie_pct}} form a band. Within a band only the band as a whole is meaningful, so a single member's axis and its extreme tokens are marked; the {{fact:x.depth.tie_pct}} line is a display convention, not a proof. Withheld: a mode whose placement equation is too unstable (condition number above {{fact:x.depth.condition}}) gives placed tokens no coordinate, so they are not drawn on that axis.

Two models side by side

{{fact:x.depth.model.pythia}} and {{fact:x.depth.model.olmo}} read exactly the same tokens, and each builds its own embedding from its own activations. Their axes are not matched: mode 2 of one need not resemble mode 2 of the other, and either may be mirrored. Compare shapes and groupings, not directions. Hovering a token rings the same token in the other plot. How far the two models' spaces agree, as spaces rather than axis by axis, is measured in the charts at the bottom.

Every index on this page

l: layer, 0 to {{fact:x.depth.last_layer}}. k: mode, 1 to {{fact:x.depth.modes_shown}} here (of {{fact:x.depth.modes_kept}} computed). p: position of a token in its text, 1 to {{fact:x.depth.text_tokens}}. c00 to c31: the Pile documents. {{fact:x.depth.first_random}} to {{fact:x.depth.last_random}}: random sequences (ids drawn uniformly from the word pieces the documents use). {{fact:x.depth.first_shuffled}} to {{fact:x.depth.last_shuffled}}: documents {{fact:x.depth.shuffled_sources}} with their tokens shuffled. Depth weight of a mode at a layer: the share of the mode that sits at that layer (the shares add up to 1 over the layers). Eigenvalue: a mode's smoothness score (smaller is smoother).
